About

My Story

It all started when i created my first Minecraft server. Of course it did terrible but it got me started with Skript. I of course did not stop using it and eventually after 1 more failed attempt one of my servers blew up. PrimalMines

I've owned 5 Minecraft servers in total. Two of which did pretty good both were PrimalMines. Season 1 did best and was around 2-3 years ago. recently i created season 2 but because i had been out of the server owner game for quite some time i based my idea of what players like on the concept of what box servers were 2-3 years ago. It did pretty decent but it had potential to be so much more. This was pretty much the final straw because i've decided to stop with the owning of servers. The parts i liked most about being a server owner was seeing players have fun. That is what continued to motivate me through making them. The part i liked the absolute most though was developing them. Creating cool systems was the most fun ever.

These days i mostly just develop some things here and then but its mostly calm.
